# Ground materials (Godot battle)

(albedo × AO + height in alpha; OpenGL normal + roughness in alpha).

Terrain3D addon (`godot/addons/terrain_3d`): MIT, Tokisan Games and contributors.
Test pack `godot/packs/ardennes`: relief from Terrarium/Mapzen elevation (AWS Open Data); land cover and
colour derived from Esri World Imagery.
